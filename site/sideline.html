Athletic trainer spotlight

What happens when a player goes down

Who they are

Athletic trainers are healthcare professionals with a master's degree and national board certification. Most states license them. They prevent, assess, treat and rehab injuries.

The first seconds

They watch every play. When a player stays down, they run on, check breathing, consciousness and the injured area, and decide whether the player can move or needs to be stabilized.

Eyes in the booth

Every NFL game also has independent certified athletic trainers watching from the press box. Since 2015 they can stop the game with a medical timeout if a player looks disoriented.

Back in or out

Return-to-play calls follow medical protocols, like the concussion protocol, not the score. The injury reports below are the public result of those decisions.
