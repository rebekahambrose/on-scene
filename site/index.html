Who responds

The people behind the dots

Dispatchers

Answer the 911 call, sort out what is happening, and send the right help. Every dot on the map starts with them.

EMS crews

EMTs and paramedics who treat patients on scene and on the way to the hospital. Most 911 calls on the map are medical.

Flight crews

Air-ambulance flight nurses and paramedics reach rural areas and critical patients when time matters most.

Athletic trainers

Licensed healthcare professionals on every sideline. When a player goes down, they make the first call on care, live on TV.
