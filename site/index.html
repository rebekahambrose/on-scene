Sources (–)

    Privacy. 911 calls are grouped into hexes about 800 m across, or counted by town, ZIP or city where a source gives no location. No addresses, names or exact times are kept.

    Air ambulances. Rounded to about 1 km, hidden below 1,000 ft, and their trails show only time in the air. Takeoff and landing spots are never shown, because they can be emergency scenes.

    What this shows. A dispatch is a call for help, not a confirmed injury. Calls cover the past 2 hours or are still active. Aircraft keep moving on their last known heading between updates.

    Sideline. Official NFL and NBA injury reports, exactly as each league published them. Every game on the map has athletic trainers from both teams on the sideline, usually the first to reach an injured player.

    Not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911.
